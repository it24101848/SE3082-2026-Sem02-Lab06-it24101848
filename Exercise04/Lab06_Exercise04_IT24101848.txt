============================================================ 6.
OBSERVATION AND COMPARISON
============================================================

Without collapse(2), OpenMP parallelizes the outer r loop. There are
five outer-loop iterations. In the observed output, thread 0 handled r =
1 and r = 2, thread 1 handled r = 3, thread 2 handled r = 4, and thread
3 handled r = 5. Each thread then executed all ten k iterations
belonging to its assigned r value.

With collapse(2), OpenMP combines the two nested loops into one logical
iteration space. Since r has 5 iterations and k has 10 iterations, the
combined iteration space contains 5 x 10 = 50 iterations.

The actual collapse(2) output demonstrates finer-grained distribution.
For example, r = 2 is divided between thread 0 and thread 1, r = 3 is
divided between thread 1 and thread 2, and r = 4 is divided between
thread 2 and thread 3. Therefore, the work is no longer distributed only
as complete outer-loop iterations.

The order of printed lines is not guaranteed because multiple OpenMP
threads execute concurrently.
